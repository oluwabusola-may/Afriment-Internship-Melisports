# Afriment-Internship-Melisports# Melisport Nigeria — Data Analyst Internship (2026) 📊

A collection of real-world data analytics work completed during my Data Analyst Internship at Melisport Nigeria — a sports brand operating across retail and community channels. Every deliverable here was built from scratch to support business decisions around market expansion, prospect targeting, and performance tracking.

---

## About the Internship

**Company:** Melisport Nigeria  
**Role:** Data Analyst Intern  
**Period:** 2026 – Present  
**Focus Areas:** Market research, prospect development, dashboard reporting, social media analytics, industry research

---

## Work Delivered

### 1. Prospect Tracker
Built a structured tracker for 15 high-potential prospects across two channels:
- **7 retail partners** — brick-and-mortar stores evaluated for product fit and reach
- **8 community partners** — sports communities and groups assessed for brand alignment

Each prospect was scored and prioritised using a consistent framework, giving the sales team a clear, data-backed shortlist to act on.

---

### 2. Market Research Report
Conducted a depth analysis of Melisport's operating landscape including:
- Competitor positioning and pricing benchmarks
- Target customer segmentation
- Channel performance comparison (retail vs community)
- Industry trends shaping the Nigerian sportswear market

Findings were structured into a formal industry research report for internal use by leadership.

---

### 3. Performance Dashboards
Developed dashboards to track key business metrics across:
- Sales pipeline activity
- Social media performance (engagement, reach, growth trends)
- Prospect conversion and outreach status

Built to be easy to update and readable by non-technical stakeholders.

---

### 4. Social Media Data Analysis
Pulled, cleaned, and analysed Melisport's social media data to surface patterns in content performance — identifying which post types, formats, and timing windows drove the most engagement.

---

### 5. Industry Research Report
Produced a structured report on the Nigerian sportswear and fitness market, covering:
- Market size and growth trajectory
- Key players and competitive dynamics
- Consumer behaviour trends
- Opportunities for Melisport's expansion strategy
